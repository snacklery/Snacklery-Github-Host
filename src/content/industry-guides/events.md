## H1: Edible Cutlery for Events: A Complete Implementation Guide

### Introduction

This guide covers edible cutlery from the event organizer and venue perspective, complementing our [Caterers guide](/learn/industry-guides/caterers), which focuses on the catering company's operational side. Event organizers — wedding planners, conference organizers, festival producers, corporate event managers — often make or influence the cutlery decision independently of, or alongside, whichever catering company is executing the event, and the considerations differ slightly depending on event type and scale.

### Executive summary (quick snapshot)

**Best products for this industry:** Varies significantly by event type — plated formats (spoons, forks, sporks) for seated dinners and weddings; stirrers for cocktail receptions; sporks for festival and outdoor food-stall settings where mixed, casual food is common.

### Coordinating with catering partners

Event organizers should clarify early in planning whether edible cutlery is sourced and managed by the event organizer directly, by the venue, or by the catering company — this affects both budgeting and who's responsible for storage, quantity planning, and on-site handling.

### FAQ

**What’s the best pilot for events?**
A smaller, seated event (private dinner, smaller wedding, or intimate corporate gathering) rather than a large-scale festival or conference.
